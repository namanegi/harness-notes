Glossary

Chunk

A piece of a document treated as one retrieval unit. Its boundaries affect which evidence arrives together.

A chunk may contain a paragraph or several paragraphs rather than the complete source. Splitting a rule from its exception can leave a retrieved chunk incomplete. In the real-document study, chapter-based reference labels are deliberately distinguished from exhaustive chunk-level relevance judgments.
