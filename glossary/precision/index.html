Glossary

Precision · P@k

How much of the returned material is relevant. In this site's P@10 metric, the denominator stays ten even if fewer results are returned.

If three of the top ten results are relevant, P@10 is 3/10 = 0.3. In the filtering experiment, keeping only those same three relevant documents still gives 0.3 with this fixed denominator. Precision among the surviving documents would instead be 3/3; that is a different measure.
