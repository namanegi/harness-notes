Glossary

nDCG · Normalized discounted cumulative gain

A ranking metric that rewards putting more relevant results earlier. The @10 suffix evaluates the first ten positions.

DCG adds relevance gains with smaller weights at later ranks; nDCG divides by the ideal ranking's DCG at the same cutoff. With nonnegative relevance labels and a positive ideal score it ranges from 0 to 1. A score of 1 matches the ideal gain, not 100% answer accuracy. The site's experiment includes known relevant documents outside the retrieved pool when calculating the ideal ranking.
