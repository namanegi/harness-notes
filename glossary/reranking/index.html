Glossary

Reranking

Reorder an already retrieved candidate set using another relevance judgment, so useful evidence appears earlier.

A reranker can promote a useful candidate that the first retriever placed too low. It cannot recover a document absent from its candidate set. Removing candidates is an additional filtering operation, with a separate risk of deleting relevant evidence.
