Glossary

Top-1 · First-result relevance

In the reranking study, the fraction of queries whose first result is labeled relevant.

Each query contributes a hit if its first result is relevant, and a miss otherwise, including when filtering leaves no result. A query with ten relevant documents still contributes at most one hit. Elsewhere, top-1 may mean classification accuracy; this glossary entry describes the retrieval metric used in the study.
