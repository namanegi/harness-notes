Jev Score

A TypeSafe question type that rates content against ordered descriptions. Its score is an expectation over level numbers, not a relevance percentage.

The reranking experiment defines four relevance levels numbered 0 through 3. The model's probabilities over those levels produce a possibly fractional score. That score can order candidates, but a value such as 2.3 is neither a 2.3% nor a 76.7% chance that a document answers the question.
