Glossary

RAG · Retrieval-augmented generation

Retrieve relevant material and provide it to a model as context for generating an answer.

A typical pipeline retrieves candidates, optionally reranks or filters them, and then generates an answer from selected evidence. The Jev reranking study evaluates retrieval and ordering only. Better retrieval metrics do not directly establish that generated answers are more accurate.
