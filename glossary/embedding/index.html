Glossary

Embedding

A numerical vector representation of content. Retrieval can compare query and document vectors to find likely relevant material.

The reranking study starts with cosine similarity between query and chunk embeddings. Similarity is a way to retrieve candidates, not proof that a chunk contains the facts needed to answer. Embedding cost is included in that study's complete experiment total.
