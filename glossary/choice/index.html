Glossary

Jev Choice

A TypeSafe question type that selects one option from a defined set, with weights for the candidate options.

The caller supplies context, instructions and labeled alternatives. In the workflow experiments, alternatives can represent actions such as searching, reading or answering; the program still performs the action. A returned candidate weight is not, by itself, evidence of calibrated correctness on a new task.
